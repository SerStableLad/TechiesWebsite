Roast #5 · May 21, 2026 · Singapore

Three builds went under the knife at GrowthLab Singapore — an agentic loop, agentic features bolted onto an app, and a virtual graph layer for AI agents. 37 of us showed up for it.

Speaker 01

Agentic Loop

Richard Lee

Walked the room through an agentic loop he's built — where the agent continuously loops through perceive, decide, act, and observe until the goal completes, rather than running a single scripted pass.

Agentic loopAutonomous agents
01

The Loop

Rather than a one-shot prompt, the agent runs an iteration cycle — taking in context, deciding a next action, acting on it, and folding the result back into the next step. The roast centred on when looping stops, how to bound runaway iteration, and how to tell the loop is converging.

Speaker 02

Agentic Features On An App

Alex Teo

Brought agentic features built on top of an existing application — layering autonomous behaviour onto a product rather than building it agent-first from scratch.

Agentic featuresProduct integration
01

Agents Bolted Onto Reality

The session grappled with retrofitting agentic capability onto an app that already ships — handling existing auth, data models, and user expectations without rebuilding the product around the agent.

Speaker 03

Evokoa

Dalton Prescott Ng

Presented Evokoa — a virtual graph layer that gives AI agents business context, letting them reason over the relationships in a company's knowledge rather than a flat pile of documents.

Virtual graph layerAI agentsBusiness context
01

A Graph Layer For Agent Business Context

Evokoa's pitch: stand up a virtual graph over existing data so agents can navigate relationships — who is responsible for what, what depends on what — the way a human at the company would, instead of dumping context into the prompt window.
