Roast #2 · April 2, 2026 · Singapore

Ee One brought Kantaro — an app that teaches Spanish through music — and asked the room to pick apart its vibe-coded AI stack. 49 of us packed SGInnovate, and the fixes poured in.

Speakers

Kantaro · kantaro.live

Ee One Lim

Presented Kantaro, an app that turns music into a Spanish-learning companion — lyrics, translations, and soundtracks pulled together by an AI stack she assembled fast. Joining her on stage: co-presenters Marcus Liang Zhu and Jesse Sng.

ClaudeGoogle Stitch (MCP)YouTubeLRCLibGemini
01

The Stack

Kantaro leans on a stack assembled mostly from AI building blocks: Claude for planning and coding, Google Stitch (via MCP) for design, YouTube as the music source, LRCLib for lyrics, and Gemini for translations — all wrapped into an experience built as a weekend project.

02

Fixes From The Room

The community left her with concrete next moves: fixes for a lyrics-syncing bug, workarounds for model-specific issues, and a set of security and scalability architecture changes before the app grows its user base.
